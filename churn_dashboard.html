Key Insights

• 26.5% overall churn rate — significant revenue loss

• Month-to-month contracts have 42.7% churn (highest)

• Electronic check users churn at 45.3%

• First 12 months are critical — most churn happens early

• Higher monthly charges correlate with higher churn

Actionable Recommendations

Offer contract incentives
Encourage month-to-month customers to switch to 1-year or 2-year contracts with discounts.
Improve payment experience
Electronic check users have highest churn — offer auto-pay discounts.
Focus on first 12 months
Implement onboarding program and early engagement campaigns.
Review pricing strategy
Analyze high monthly charges — consider loyalty discounts.
